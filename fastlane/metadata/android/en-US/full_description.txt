SemplicePhone replaces the home screen with a few large, clear tiles for people who rarely use a smartphone. A family member sets it up once; the senior only sees what they need.

<b>Every tile is customizable</b>
<ul>
<li>Direct call to a contact, with their photo</li>
<li>WhatsApp video call</li>
<li>Open an app, a website or a shortcut</li>
<li>Pre-written SMS</li>
<li>Large clock and date</li>
<li>SOS button</li>
</ul>

<b>SOS</b>: long press, cancellable countdown, then a call to the first number and the location sent to family.

<b>Reminders for medicines and activities</b>: shower, shopping, appointments, card games — every day, on chosen days or on one date, with symbol and photo. Full-screen alarm even on the lock screen; repeats until confirmed.

<b>Safer calls</b>: block unknown numbers, automatic speakerphone, spoken confirmation before calling, a missed-calls tile to call back with one tap, and a "Search apps" tile to find and open an app by typing or saying its name. The phone says who is calling and answers family calls by itself on speakerphone.

<b>Weather, photos, bedside</b>: weather tile, family photo slideshow tile, big clock while charging.

<b>Messages, radio, quiet night</b>: a tile with unread messages (SMS, WhatsApp) read aloud; radio with favourite stations and huge buttons. At night only SOS numbers ring; in the evening it reminds to charge the phone. Tiles can open only when held, against accidental touches; high contrast or dark theme in the evening.

<b>Ready-made layouts and album</b>: the first time you pick the situation (independent, calls only, memory difficulties, poor eyesight, hard of hearing, shaky hands). The album also shows photos and videos from the phone's folders; the family can send full-screen video messages.

<b>Against scams</b>: alert when a remote-control app is installed (AnyDesk, TeamViewer…), links from strangers flagged, premium and foreign calls blocked, alert on long calls with strangers. Fall detection, rescuers' card, torch and magnifier.

<b>No getting lost</b>: a "Home" button stays visible inside other apps; one tap goes back to the simple screen.

<b>Protection</b>: PIN-protected settings, ringer volume that restores itself, low-battery alert.

<b>Optional remote management</b>: a family member edits the tiles from a self-hosted PHP portal (included). Configurations are signed with Ed25519; the portal receives SOS, missed-reminder and battery alerts, also via Telegram or email.

Languages: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish.

No ads, no tracking, no account. Free software (GPL-3.0).
